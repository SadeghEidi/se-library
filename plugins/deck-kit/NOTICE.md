`kit/fonts/TTDrugs-*.otf`. A commercial retail family from TypeType, bundled
here so the `midnight` and `daylight` themes render as designed.

**It is not covered by this repository's MIT licence, and installing this
plugin does not license it to you.** If you intend to use those two themes in
something you publish or present commercially, buy a licence from TypeType
(https://typetype.org). If you would rather not think about it, use the
`neutral` theme: it is Inter Tight only, and open all the way down.
